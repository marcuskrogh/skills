# Retro-futuristic direction

Default **direction** for [CONCEPT_FRONTEND](CONCEPT_FRONTEND.md) when the brief
leaves stance free. Load from that concept or from a skill that pins this look.

## Stance

Design as an optimistic 1960s tomorrow through **Dieter Rams** / Braun: pale
housing, a modular grid, one analog meter. Useful, understandable, unobtrusive,
honest, as little design as possible. Not a mustard pavilion, not joke Googie,
not a dark phosphor cockpit.

Name the era in the token plan (one sentence), then apply it to this
**subject**. Example: *1962 marine instrument — pale housing, modular grid,
orange tide needle.* The era is a lens on the **subject**, not a costume from
another world.

## Tokens

- **Color.** Pale cream hull. Quiet sand for modules and hairlines. One warm
  orange for the **signature** and the primary action. Ink is near-black, warm.
  Four to six named values. Sand is architecture; orange is the instrument.
- **Type.** **Archivo** for display and body (a close square grotesk if Archivo
  is unavailable). Tracked labels. Tabular numerals on readings. Skip novelty
  display faces. High-contrast serif on cream is the editorial cluster, not
  this stance.
- **Layout.** A 606-like board: aligned modules, square flush controls, tight
  gutters, almost no radius. The **signature** is one analog meter (circular
  face in a square housing, thin needle) — not a CRT, not a coloured well, not
  a row of cards.
- **Motion.** A needle settle, or none. `transform` / `opacity` only. Still when
  `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Intensity

Less, but fully built. Every remaining control is aligned and labelled. Do not
fill the plane with colour fields or oversized wells. Surrounding architecture
stays one family (same radius, same label style) so the **signature** still
reads as the one memorable thing.

## Distinct from default clusters

| Cluster | This stance |
|---------|-------------|
| Editorial: cream, serif display, terracotta | Pale hull, geometric type, orange instrument |
| Terminal: near-black, acid-green or vermilion | Light housing, analog meter, no phosphor |
