# Retro-futuristic direction

Default **direction** for [CONCEPT_FRONTEND](CONCEPT_FRONTEND.md) when the brief
leaves stance free. Load from that concept or from a skill that pins this look.

## Stance

Design as an optimistic 1960s–70s tomorrow through **Dieter Rams** / Braun
**household instruments**: the Sixtant-class shaver and the Aromaster coffee
machine. Off-white plastic housing, brushed steel, one vivid orange control.
Rounded capsule volumes. Air between groups: one job per module. Useful,
understandable, unobtrusive, honest, as little design as possible.

Name the era in the token plan (one sentence), then apply it to this
**subject**. Example: *1972 marine instrument — off-white hull, steel tide
window, orange slack switch.* The era is a lens on the **subject**, not a
costume from another world.

## Tokens

- **Color.** Off-white hull (appliance plastic). Cool steel for rims and
  seams. One vivid Braun orange for the **signature** switch and the one
  live value. Ink is near-black, warm. Four to six named values. Off-white
  is the body; steel is the working edge; orange is the control.
- **Type.** **Archivo** for display and body (a close square grotesk if Archivo
  is unavailable). Label above the value. Tracked labels. Tabular numerals on
  readings. Skip novelty display faces. High-contrast serif on cream is the
  editorial cluster, not this stance.
- **Layout.** Capsule modules with generous radius and gutters. Separate
  groups (status, reading, controls, schedule) into their own modules or
  stacked blocks. The **signature** is the orange switch plus a steel-window
  numeric readout — type in a rounded steel frame, not a circular dial, not
  a needle, not a CRT, not a coloured well.
- **Motion.** None, or a brief opacity on a new row. `transform` / `opacity`
  only. Still when `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Intensity

Less, but fully built. Space between groups is part of the design. Every
remaining control is aligned and labelled. Volume comes from radius and
steel, not from packed numerals. Surrounding architecture stays one family
so the **signature** still reads as the one memorable thing.

## Distinct from default clusters

| Cluster | This stance |
|---------|-------------|
| Editorial: cream, serif display, terracotta | Off-white plastic, geometric type, orange switch |
| Terminal: near-black, acid-green or vermilion | Light housing, steel window, no phosphor |
