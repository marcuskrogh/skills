# Retro-futuristic direction

Default **direction** for [CONCEPT_FRONTEND](CONCEPT_FRONTEND.md) when the brief
leaves stance free. Load from that concept or from a skill that pins this look.

## Stance

Design as an optimistic 1960s–70s tomorrow through **Dieter Rams** / Braun
**household instruments**: the Sixtant-class shaver and the Aromaster coffee
machine. Off-white plastic housing, brushed steel, one vivid orange control.
Rounded, capsule volumes. Useful, understandable, unobtrusive, honest, as
little design as possible.

Name the era in the token plan (one sentence), then apply it to this
**subject**. Example: *1972 marine instrument — off-white hull, steel tide
head, orange slack switch.* The era is a lens on the **subject**, not a
costume from another world.

## Tokens

- **Color.** Off-white hull (appliance plastic). Cool steel for rims, tracks,
  and instrument heads. One vivid Braun orange for the **signature** needle
  and the primary switch. Ink is near-black, warm. Four to six named values.
  Off-white is the body; steel is the working head; orange is the control.
- **Type.** **Archivo** for display and body (a close square grotesk if Archivo
  is unavailable). Tracked labels. Tabular numerals on readings. Skip novelty
  display faces. High-contrast serif on cream is the editorial cluster, not
  this stance.
- **Layout.** Capsule modules: generous radius, aligned gutters, circular
  steel housings. The **signature** is one analog meter (circular steel-rimmed
  face in a rounded off-white body, thin orange needle) — not a CRT, not a
  coloured well, not a row of cards, not a square 606 grid.
- **Motion.** A needle settle, or none. `transform` / `opacity` only. Still when
  `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Intensity

Less, but fully built. Every remaining control is aligned and labelled. Volume
comes from radius and steel, not from colour fields. Surrounding architecture
stays one family (same radius, same label style) so the **signature** still
reads as the one memorable thing.

## Distinct from default clusters

| Cluster | This stance |
|---------|-------------|
| Editorial: cream, serif display, terracotta | Off-white plastic, geometric type, orange switch |
| Terminal: near-black, acid-green or vermilion | Light housing, steel head, analog meter |
