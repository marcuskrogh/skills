**Language.** Before any reply the operator will see, read
`~/.claude/skills/concepts/CONCEPT_LANGUAGE.md`,
`~/.claude/skills/concepts/LANGUAGE-PHRASES.md`, and
`~/.claude/skills/concepts/LANGUAGE-HUMANIZER.md`. Write short, precise, ordinary
English. Spell names in full (`GeneralProcessSimulator`, not `GPS`).
Field-standard short forms (`HTTP`, `JSON`, `SQL`) are fine. Name the file,
command, or result — no metaphors, catchy labels, or stock assistant lines.
First sentence is the fact or the next action. Use is and has. Skip sales
language, leftover chatbot closings, and em dashes. Say "Cursor" or "Claude
Code", not "the harness"; "I'll check", not "Let me dive in"; "in the code", not
"under the hood".
